01 / ABOUTHELLO, TRAINER!

WHAT IS
MILKYMON?

welcome to milkymon: regions, a minecraft survival multiplayer server built around the mod cobblemon ↗.

gameplay is modeled around the world of pokemon, where players are split into four different regions, each with their own unique culture, lore, and identity.

throughout the season, regions compete in server-wide events, earn points, work together on builds + quests, and shape the world around them.

curious about what we're up to? say hi on twitter! ↗

other links: read the wiki ↗ | meet the staff team ↗

02 / REGIONSOUR LOVELY COMMUNITIES

MEET THE
REGIONS.

LED BY SILVER & STARSSERGI𖦹

VALHALLA

previously known as the city of knowledge, damaris held too many secrets. secrets that were far more trouble than they were worth. now, it is the city of endless dunes. descendants of the lost city return after centuries to rebuild what was once there, but stronger. valhalla, the city of warriors now takes its place. sitting along the blue sea in the desert and blessed by the gods, connecting with the creatures of the land came quickly.

LED BY ZOMGOAT, PLUSHYYOSHI, GOOSIE❆

HOKUNDRA

hokundra is a harsh and unforgiving land. its tundra is cold, barren, and difficult to survive in. in the earliest days of this region, a fortress was set up to protect its people from the dangers of the wilderness and any nefarious forces that may threaten the people.

hokundra was established under the guidance of the lake guardians: uxie, mesprit, and azelf. each guardian sought to bring humans to a region that embodied one of their defining traits: curiosity and the pursuit of knowledge, empathy and emotional connection, endurance and the strength to persevere.

LED BY CADV & VINH KY⚓︎

BAYLANDS

the settlements of the baylands were originally established by groups of pokémon researchers determined to explore one question: how well can humans and pokémon truly coexist?

the experiments in coexistence have been surprisingly successful as the settlements they established have continued to grow. over time, the pokémon that originally lived alongside the researchers have become a core part of the region’s culture, influencing everything from its communities to its methods of transportation. today, the baylands are home to thriving settlements of pokémon and humans that coexist in harmony.

LED BY PROJECTTEMPO & KRANK🐟︎

KARP KINGDOM

blessed by a meteorite that contained the stone capable of unlocking gyarados' true potential, king tempo founded the kingdom upon the strength and legacy of the great dragon. the people of karp kingdom believe they are the descendants of this power. they are not merely trainers. they are karps.
