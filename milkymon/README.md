

# a quiz for milkymon:regions!
## find what region you *really* belong to.

> ilkymon: regions is a minecraft survival multiplayer server built around the mod cobblemon!
> gameplay is modeled around the world of pokemon, where players are split into four different regions, each with their own unique culture, lore, and identity. events are hosted across the entire playerbase, where players will compete against each other and earn points for their region.

-# disclaimer for wave 2 players: this quiz is not meant to influence your region choices. it’s just for funsies!
